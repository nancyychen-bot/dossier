# Dossier — A Private CRM for the People You Meet

Dossier is a personal contact catalog built for speed of capture and richness of detail. Designed as "a private dossier for forgetful people," it lets you record who you met, what you talked about, and what to follow up on — by voice or text — so you never lose track of a connection again. Built with Next.js, Notion as the backend, and Claude for AI-powered search and email drafting, it turns scattered impressions into a curated, searchable roster you actually maintain.

## Key Features

- **Voice capture & meeting logging** — speak a name, role, and context into the mic and Dossier parses it into a structured contact record or meeting entry
- **AI-powered semantic search** — find people by natural-language queries like "the designer I met at the conference in Tokyo" instead of exact-match keywords
- **Inline editing & bulk operations** — update roles, companies, cities, and tags across multiple contacts at once directly from the list view
- **Follow-up email drafts** — generates contextual follow-up emails with Claude based on each contact's profile and meeting history
- **CSV export** — download your entire roster for backup or use in other tools
